# stack-k8s 1.1.7

Three planes move together. The gateway, its `focus-export` sidecar and the
control plane (tokenfuse `v1.0.4`) no longer follow a provider redirect, so
the caller's key and prompt cannot follow a 307 to another host, and retain a
reservation when a provider send fails after the connection existed, instead
of releasing it while the provider may still have received the call. wardryx (`v1.0.3`) answers readiness by pinging its own
store under a bound, and bounds a policy write so it fails with a 503 rather
than hanging past that bound. heraldyx (`v0.2.5`, opt-in) describes a stalled
run without guessing its cause, and stops promising a refund for a failed
provider call.

## The pinned set, 2026-09-23

As in 1.1.6, with `heraldyx:v0.2.5` in place of v0.2.3, `wardryx:v1.0.3` in
place of v1.0.2, and `tokenfuse:v1.0.4` (twice) and
`tokenfuse-control-plane:v1.0.4` in place of v1.0.3. No other image moves.

## What to know on the way in

- wardryx now exposes `GET /readyz`, pinging its store under a 3 second
  timeout. No new required env var and no changed default: `components.json`
  carries the new `ready_path` field, everything else is unchanged. This
  manifest set does not wire a Kubernetes readiness probe to it; that stays a
  possible follow-up, out of scope for a version-only re-pin.
- The gateway no longer releases a reservation on an ambiguous provider send
  failure (one of the two limits named in 1.1.6). The other, clean streams
  without final usage, remains under review. A connect error still releases.
- A `TOKENFUSE_UPSTREAM` that relied on the gateway following a redirect must
  point at the final address.
- heraldyx's `run_stalled` wording and its failed-call wording both moved;
  neither changes a wire shape or an env var.

## Not in this release, said plainly

- No cluster was run on this pinned set. The 1.1.0 cluster run and these
  static gates do not prove the new combination on a live cluster.
- heraldyx and tokenfuse's `components.json` carry no changes between their
  old and new refs (no new or removed env var, no port, volume or user
  change); wardryx's only change is the new `ready_path` field above. No
  wiring in `00-base.yaml`'s `stack-wiring` ConfigMap or any Secret becomes
  stale for this re-pin.
