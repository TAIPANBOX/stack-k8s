# stack-k8s 1.18.1

A patch release. Every container that reads the whole events bus gets at least 1Gi of memory: the keyryx witness (512Mi before), the chain verifier agent-conform (256Mi), heraldyx (128Mi), the record seal trailryx and idryx-rescan (512Mi each). Requests are unchanged, so nothing more is reserved.

## Upgrade first

1. **Re-run the launcher, or apply the manifests.** The witness, heraldyx and the three CronJobs pick up the new limit; no image moves, no setting changes.
2. **Why.** The bus is never rotated, so what a reader of every stream holds grows with the cluster's age. On a single box (stack-single 1.14.0, 2026-11-25) the witness was killed at its 256 MiB ceiling in a loop while it re-hashed the bus. The limits here were higher and had not failed, but the failure is the same one, later.
3. **Going back to 1.18.0** needs nothing special.

## What changed

- **Memory limits for the bus readers** (invariant 96): `scripts/bus-readers-have-room.sh` finds each of the five by name and refuses one below 1Gi, with four teeth cases and `features/every-bus-reader-has-room.feature`.
