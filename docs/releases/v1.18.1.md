# stack-k8s 1.18.1

A patch release. Every container that reads the whole events bus gets at least 1Gi of memory: the keyryx witness (512Mi before), the chain verifier agent-conform (256Mi), heraldyx (128Mi), the record seal trailryx and idryx-rescan (512Mi each). Requests are unchanged, so nothing more is reserved. TokenFuse moves to v1.27.0.

## Upgrade first

1. **Re-run the launcher, or apply the manifests.** The witness, heraldyx and the three CronJobs pick up the new limit, and the gateway (with its focus-export sidecar), the MCP broker and the control plane move to TokenFuse v1.27.0; the console image built on this tag carries it as its backtest engine. The new limits in TokenFuse v1.27.0 are off by default and the manifests do not turn them on. No setting changes.
2. **Why.** The bus is never rotated, so what a reader of every stream holds grows with the cluster's age. On a single box (stack-single 1.14.0, 2026-11-25) the witness was killed at its 256 MiB ceiling in a loop while it re-hashed the bus. The limits here were higher and had not failed, but the failure is the same one, later.
3. **Going back to 1.18.0** needs nothing special.

## What changed

- **TokenFuse v1.27.0**: `tokenfuse` v1.26.0 `7080f921` to v1.27.0 `9ada89b4` and `tokenfuse-control-plane` v1.26.0 `a0f292a0` to v1.27.0 `d8ad6e9a`, by tag and digest read from ghcr.io, in the manifests, the console image's engine stage and label, `scripts/tokenfuse-door-origins.sh` and the teeth cases that name a pin.

- **Memory limits for the bus readers** (invariant 96): `scripts/bus-readers-have-room.sh` finds each of the five by name and refuses one below 1Gi, with four teeth cases and `features/every-bus-reader-has-room.feature`.
