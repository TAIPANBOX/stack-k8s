# stack-k8s 1.1.29

A fix, no pin moves: every opt-in workload now leaves a dead node in 30 s,
and the dead-node gate judges every manifest.

## What changes

- **Eight opt-in workloads carry invariant 21's tolerations** (#130).
  - The workloads: `hub-ingress`, `typryx`, `tokenfuse-mcp-broker`,
    `typryx-wardryx-proxy`, `heraldyx`, `scopyx`, `scopyx-browser` and
    `costcrew`. Each tolerates `node.kubernetes.io/unreachable` and
    `node.kubernetes.io/not-ready` for 30 s instead of Kubernetes' default
    300 s.
  - The broker and the risk proxy, the two that roll and serve a port, also
    sleep 5 s in `preStop`.
- **The gate's subjects are every `manifests/*.yaml`.**
  `scripts/planes-leave-a-dead-node.sh` used to take its subjects from
  `kustomization.yaml`, so no opt-in manifest was ever judged. It now finds
  15 workloads where it found 6. Two teeth cases and two bound scenarios
  were added. GOTCHAS 119 records it.
- **`cloud/**/*.plan` is ignored.** A saved Terraform plan can carry values
  from the operator's account and does not belong in the tree.

No image pin moves; the set is v1.1.28's.

## Measured

- **The outage this fixes.** On a GCP hub from v1.1.27 (2026-10-05), the node
  carrying wardryx, policy-db and `hub-ingress` was stopped. A remote site was
  refused fail-closed for 436 s. About 4.5 minutes of that was `hub-ingress`
  waiting the default 300 s, while wardryx and policy-db moved on their 30 s.
- **The gate goes red first.** The widened gate on the old manifests reported
  10 problems across 15 workloads. After the fix, `gates-have-teeth.sh`
  passed 188 of 188 and all 26 gates were green.
- **#360 through a stack broker.** The same day, a `tokenfuse:v1.6.1` broker
  on k3d served openwrt-mcp 0.5.0 end to end: initialize, tools/list and
  tools/call with SSE replies and its session id. A `v1.5.0` broker answered
  `bad upstream json`.

## Not proven

- **The outage window with the fix.** It is expected near 3 minutes and was
  not re-run.
- **A moved entry still re-requests its two certificates.** Its ACME state is
  an `emptyDir`, a named limitation in `53-hub-entry.yaml`. Each move costs
  about 70 s and two Let's Encrypt issuances.
- **This exact set on a cluster.** `verify.sh` and `security-tests.sh` were
  not run against it.
