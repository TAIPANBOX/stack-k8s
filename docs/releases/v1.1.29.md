# stack-k8s 1.1.29

No pin moves. One fix merged since 1.1.28 ships: the opt-in workloads now leave
a dead node in 30 s, and the gate that guards it judges every manifest.

## What changes

- **Opt-in workloads leave a dead node in 30 s** (#130, invariant 21,
  GOTCHAS 119).
  - The eight workloads an installer flag or a script applies on their own
    (hub-ingress, typryx, tokenfuse-mcp-broker, typryx-wardryx-proxy,
    heraldyx, scopyx, scopyx-browser, costcrew) now carry 30 s NoExecute
    tolerations for `unreachable` and `not-ready`. Before, they waited the
    Kubernetes default of 300 s.
  - The MCP broker and the typed risk proxy, the two of them that roll and
    serve a port, have a 5 s `preStop` sleep.
  - `scripts/planes-leave-a-dead-node.sh` now takes its subjects from every
    `manifests/*.yaml`, found rather than listed. It used to read only
    `manifests/kustomization.yaml`, so none of the eight was ever judged. A
    patch file has no top-level kind and is skipped.

## Measured

The fault was measured on a GCP hub on 2026-10-05, on stack-k8s v1.1.27
through `deploy-gcp.sh` and `hub/up.sh`, three `e2-standard-2`. A remote site's
gateway asked the hub's wardryx on every call (no cache, fail closed), and the
node carrying wardryx, policy-db and hub-ingress was stopped. The site was
refused for 436 s, about 4.5 minutes of it hub-ingress waiting out the default
toleration. Every refusal was a fail-closed 403; nothing passed undecided.

On the change itself: the widened gate fails on the unchanged manifests (10
problems across 15 workloads, where the old gate said OK over 6);
`gates-have-teeth.sh` passes 188 cases, two of them new;
`typed/mode.sh render --with-typed --typed-risk-signal` renders 3 Deployments
with 6 tolerations and 2 `preStop` hooks.

## Not proven

- **The outage with this fix on a live cluster.** It is expected to fall to
  about 3 minutes and was not re-run.
- **A moved entry still re-requests its two certificates.** Its ACME state is
  an emptyDir (53's own header): about 70 s and two Let's Encrypt issuances
  per move.
- **One replica is still an outage** while the new pod starts.
- **This exact set on a cluster.** `verify.sh` and `security-tests.sh` were not
  run against it.
