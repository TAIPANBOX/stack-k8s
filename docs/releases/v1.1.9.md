# stack-k8s 1.1.9

One securityContext field, no image moves. The pinned set is exactly
1.1.8's.

## What changed

The wardryx pod joins the stack group: `fsGroup: 10001` beside its own
`runAsUser` and `runAsGroup` 65532 (#94). The shared `stack-events` volume
is written by tokenfuse-cloud and the console, whose `fsGroup: 10001` leaves
it owned by group 10001 with mode 2775, and wardryx appends its own event
log there. As 65532:65532 with no supplementary group that append was
refused. `fsGroup` adds group 10001 to the process whatever the volume
type. The single-box launcher already runs wardryx as `65532:10001`.

## Upgrade

`kubectl apply -k manifests`. The wardryx pod restarts once with the new
securityContext; nothing else moves.

## Not in this release, said plainly

- Not measured on Longhorn RWX: whether its share honours `fsGroup` or is
  exported writable to everyone. In the second case this change has no
  effect and does no harm.
- The full stack was not re-applied with this change. What ran: a refused
  write as 65532:65532 and an accepted one with group 10001 added, and a pod
  with exactly the new securityContext on a local kind cluster reporting
  group 10001 and writing to a group-10001 directory.
