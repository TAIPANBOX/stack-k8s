# stack-k8s 1.16.0

A minor release that moves the record seal to trailryx-node v1.6.0. The record plane now writes a closing record for a hold wardryx closed by its lifetime (`approval_expired`, read as a timeout) or that its own agent withdrew (`approval_withdrawn`, read as no verdict); under v1.5.0 both were refused as unknown types. Neither reads as a denial. The image also carries the lockfile that clears every advisory with a reachable fix.

## Upgrade first

1. **Re-run the launcher, or apply the manifests of this tag.** No new object, no changed claim, no new setting. One image moves by tag and digest:

   | Image | 1.15.0 | 1.16.0 |
   |---|---|---|
   | trailryx-node (the record seal) | v1.5.0 `f15215cd` | v1.6.0 `68c413c2` |

   The seal's Keyryx signer, its pinned key and its credential are unchanged.
2. **Going back to 1.15.0 needs nothing special.** Apply the 1.15.0 manifests. Records written under v1.6.0 carry mapper version 115 and stay valid; v1.5.0 refuses the two types again from the next line it reads.

## What changed

- **The record seal pin** moves to trailryx-node v1.6.0 in `manifests/40-routines-and-secrets.yaml`. The gates that name the pin (`keyryx-release-pins.sh` and its teeth case) read the new one.

## Evidence

- CI on this release's pull request: `gates`, `fast-gates` and the six teeth shards.
- The digest was read from the registry with `docker buildx imagetools inspect`, and its provenance was verified with `gh attestation verify -R TAIPANBOX/trailryx`.
