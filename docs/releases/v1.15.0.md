# stack-k8s 1.15.0

A minor release that pins the console 1.14.0 published. The console now shows a hold wardryx closed by its lifetime as "Expired, nobody decided in time" and one its agent withdrew as "Withdrawn by the agent", in a neutral colour, where v1.12.0 showed both in the refusal colour. Nothing else moves.

## Upgrade first

1. **Re-run the launcher, or apply the manifests of this tag.** No new object, no changed claim, no new setting. One image moves by tag and digest:

   | Image | 1.14.0 | 1.15.0 |
   |---|---|---|
   | genaryx-console (the console, `verdryx-drift`, `verdryx-slo`) | v1.12.0 `32c28d24` | v1.14.0 `583fcbe1` |

   It is built from genaryx `cb7f779` with the engines 1.14.0 pins (tokenfuse v1.25.0, wardryx v2.8.0). The preview key, the approver key and every setting the console reads are unchanged.
2. **Going back to 1.14.0 needs nothing special.** Apply the 1.14.0 manifests; the console then shows expired and withdrawn holds in the refusal colour again, and decides every pending hold as before.

## What changed

- **The console pin** moves to v1.14.0 in the console, the two quality CronJobs and the security tests. The gates that name the pin (the console cases of `gates-have-teeth.sh`) read the new one.

## Evidence

- CI on this release's pull request: `gates`, `fast-gates` and the six teeth shards.
- The console digest was read from the registry with `docker buildx imagetools inspect` (amd64 and arm64), and its provenance was verified with `gh attestation verify -R TAIPANBOX/stack-k8s`.
